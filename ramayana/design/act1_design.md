# Act I — The Birth of Rama: Design Sheets

Source: [ramayana_complete_illustrative_retelling.txt](../ramayana_complete_illustrative_retelling.txt) lines 130–285.
Target: ~60–75 min total. Checkpoint after every objective. Virtue meter: **Duty / Loyalty / Compassion / Courage**.

---

## Chapter 1 — Ayodhya, City of a King (Prologue, ~8 min)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Story beat | Childless Dasharatha performs the ritual; a divine being rises from the fire with a sacred offering; four sons are born; the city celebrates |
| Playable | Dasharatha (Old King form) |
| NPCs | Vasishta, Kausalya, Kaikeyi, Sumitra, priests, courtiers, citizens |
| Scene | Palace at dawn, oil lamps on stone corridors, chanting, then city waking up: garlands, musicians, decorated royal elephants |
| Mood | Quiet sorrow → hope → celebration |

**Objectives (in order)**
1. *Walk the palace corridors to the council* — movement tutorial (left/right, doors, stairs); slow, heavy walk animation for the old king.
2. *Speak with Vasishta* — dialogue tutorial; decision to perform the ritual.
3. *Attend the sacred fire* — cinematic: the divine being and the offering (Timeline cutscene, no gameplay).
4. *Wait in the palace courtyard* — ambient: news of four births arrives one by one (Rama, Bharata, Lakshmana & Shatrughna).
5. *Hold the newborn Rama* — emotional close-up; camera zooms out through parallax layers over celebrating Ayodhya → title card.

**Unlocks:** Codex — Ayodhya, Dasharatha, the four queens.
**Virtue moments:** none (prologue).

## Chapter 2 — Four Brothers (~15 min)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Story beat | Rama grows into the eldest prince: studies Vedas, statecraft, ethics, weapons; bond with Lakshmana; people love him |
| Playable | Rama (Young Prince form, ~teen) |
| NPCs | Lakshmana, Bharata, Shatrughna, Vasishta (teacher), Dasharatha, citizens |
| Scene | Gurukul courtyard, royal archery range, Ayodhya market streets |

**Objectives**
1. *Archery lesson* — aim/shoot tutorial with aim-assist; hit 5 targets.
2. *Spar with Lakshmana* — dodge + melee basics; Lakshmana is fast and eager (shows his personality).
3. *Race Bharata and Shatrughna to the river gate* — sprint/jump/climb traversal tutorial.
4. *Walk through the city* — 3 optional citizen requests (help a fallen cart, settle a dispute, return a lost child). Each gives **Duty/Compassion**.
5. *Evening with Dasharatha* — he speaks of succession (foreshadowing).

**Unlocks:** Basic Bow, Dodge, Sprint.
**Enemies:** none (training dummies only).

## Chapter 3 — The Arrival of Vishvamitra (~12 min)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Story beat | Vishvamitra asks for Rama to protect his sacrifice; Dasharatha refuses then yields; Rama and Lakshmana leave; the sage teaches them sacred knowledge and gives them weapons |
| Playable | Rama (Young Prince) + Lakshmana companion (AI, first time) |
| NPCs | Vishvamitra, Dasharatha, Vasishta |
| Scene | Royal court → Sarayu riverbank path → forest edge at dusk |

**Objectives**
1. *Court cinematic* — Vishvamitra's request, Dasharatha's refusal, Vasishta supports the sage.
2. *Choose your farewell* — say goodbye to Kausalya / Sumitra / Dasharatha (dialogue order is free; outcome is fixed). **Loyalty** for speaking to all.
3. *Journey with the sage* — traversal along the river; Lakshmana follows (companion AI intro).
4. *Learn Bala and Atibala* — short meditation mini-game (hold + release rhythm).
5. *Receive the divine weapons* — cinematic + first astra unlock.

**Unlocks:** Stamina boost (Bala/Atibala), **Astra slot 1**, companion command "Lakshmana, cover!".

## Chapter 4 — Tataka and the Forest of Tests (~20 min, first boss)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Story beat | In a dark, threatening forest Rama meets Tataka; he hesitates; Vishvamitra explains the larger duty; Rama acts; the sacrifice is protected |
| Playable | Rama (Young Prince) + Lakshmana AI |
| NPCs | Vishvamitra |
| Enemies | Forest shadow-beasts (minor), rakshasa raiders led by **Subahu** and **Maricha** (from Valmiki) |
| Boss | **Tataka** |
| Scene | Night forest: huge tree shadows, unfamiliar sounds, fog, fireflies; then hermitage altar clearing |

**Objectives**
1. *Enter the forest* — atmosphere + first real combat vs small creatures; learn target lock.
2. *Follow the sage's path* — sound-cue navigation in darkness.
3. *Confront Tataka* — pre-fight dialogue presents Rama's hesitation and the sage's explanation (canon).
4. **Boss: Tataka** (3 phases)
   - P1: throws boulders/uproots trees → dodge + shoot.
   - P2: vanishes into darkness, illusions → track by sound and Lakshmana's call-outs.
   - P3: enraged charge → use Astra 1 when she is stunned.
5. *Defend the sacrifice* — Vishvamitra begins the six-day ritual; 3 waves of raiders attack the altar (protect-objective with health bar).
6. **Mini-boss: Subahu and Maricha** — Subahu attacks from the ground (defeated); Maricha attacks from the sky and is struck by Rama's arrow that hurls him far away across the ocean — he survives. Sets up his return as the Golden Deer in Chapter 15.

**Unlocks:** Target lock, Astra 2, Codex — Tataka, Vishvamitra.
**Virtue:** protect the altar without letting it drop below 50% → **Duty**; revive Lakshmana quickly → **Loyalty**.
**Rating note (15+):** stylised combat, no gore; defeat shown with dissolving dark smoke.
**Decision:** Subahu and Maricha are added from Valmiki (not named in the retelling) because Maricha's escape pays off in Chapter 15.

## Chapter 5 — Ahalya (~10 min)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Story beat | Rama enters Ahalya's isolated hermitage; his arrival ends her long suffering; a story of restoration |
| Playable | Rama (Young Prince) + Lakshmana + Vishvamitra (walk-along) |
| NPCs | Ahalya (Gautama in memory only) |
| Scene | Silent, grey, overgrown hermitage frozen in time; colour returns as the chapter progresses |

**Objectives**
1. *Enter the silent hermitage* — no combat; grey, desaturated world.
2. *Restore the path* — light puzzle: follow faint traces and clear the overgrown path; colour and sound return step by step.
3. *Meet Ahalya* — respectful cinematic; no gameplay, no scoring.
4. *The road to Mithila* — walk out; world in full colour; tease of Mithila on the horizon → Act I end card with the chapter's meaning.

**Unlocks:** Codex — Ahalya; Act II.
**Virtue:** **Compassion** for completing the full restoration path.
